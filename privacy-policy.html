Who we are

C&I Media is a done-for-you YouTube agency operated by 436 Roosevelt Inc. (“C&I Media”, “we”, “us” or “our”). This Privacy Policy applies when you visit c-and-i-media.com (the “Site”), fill in our forms, book a call, sign up for text messages, or work with us as a client.

We handle personal information in line with Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA), and we give the same care to visitors from other countries.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Business details you share in our qualifying form or on a call, such as your company, website, YouTube channel, niche, offer, goals and budget range.
  • Booking details such as the date, time and time zone of your call and any notes you add when scheduling.
  • Messages you send us by email, text message, phone or video call.
  • Client materials you provide while we work together, such as recordings, footage, brand assets, scripts and any account access you choose to grant (for example to your YouTube channel).
  • Billing details such as your billing name and address. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage data such as your IP address, browser type, operating system, the pages you view, the page that referred you, and the date and time of your visit.
  • Video viewing data from our embedded video player (for example whether a video was played and how much of it was watched).
  • Cookies and local storage, described in section 7.

Information from other sources

  • Our form and scheduling tools pass us what you submit through them.
  • Publicly available information about your business or channel (for example public YouTube statistics) that we review to prepare for your call.

How we use your information

  • To answer your questions, assess whether we are a good fit, and prepare for and hold your call.
  • To schedule calls and send confirmations, reminders and follow-ups by email or, if you opted in, by text message.
  • To deliver our services: strategy, ideation, scripting, editing, thumbnails, SEO and publishing.
  • To manage contracts, invoices, payments and client communication.
  • To send you news and offers about our services, only where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To understand how the Site is used so we can improve it.
  • To keep the Site and our systems secure, prevent fraud or misuse, comply with the law and enforce our Terms & Conditions.

We collect only what we need for these purposes. We will not use your information for a new purpose you would not reasonably expect without telling you and, where required, asking for your consent.

Text messages (SMS)

If you give us your mobile number and opt in on our SMS opt-in page, or on another form that shows our SMS consent wording, we use that number to send the text messages you agreed to: call confirmations and reminders, replies to your enquiry, project updates for clients, and — only if you separately agreed to them — occasional promotional messages about our services.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any message to opt out, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is never a condition of buying from us.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing with subcontractors in support services, such as the messaging platform that delivers our texts or customer service, is permitted. All other categories of sharing in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Full details of the messaging program are in our SMS terms.

Consent

We collect, use and disclose personal information with your consent, which may be express or implied depending on the information and the purpose, or as otherwise permitted or required by law. When you submit a form, book a call or become a client, you consent to the uses described for that purpose. We always ask for express consent before sending you text messages or marketing emails.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual limits: use the unsubscribe link in any marketing email, reply STOP to any text message, or email us. If you withdraw consent we may not be able to provide some services, and we will tell you if that is the case.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only as follows:

  • Service providers that help us run our business and act on our behalf: website hosting, forms (Typeform), scheduling (Calendly), video hosting (Wistia), web fonts (Google Fonts), email and text messaging platforms, payment processing, accounting, and the project-management and file-sharing tools we use to deliver client work. They may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • Team members and contractors, such as editors, designers and scriptwriters who work on your project, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Platforms you ask us to use, for example when we upload videos to your YouTube channel. Those platforms handle the information under their own policies.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when we believe disclosure is required by law, court order or a government request, or is needed to protect our rights, property or safety, or that of others.
  • Business transfers, if C&I Media or 436 Roosevelt Inc. is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets, in which case the receiving party must protect your information as described here.

None of the categories above includes text messaging originator opt-in data and consent, which is handled as described in section 4.

Cookies and local storage

The Site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Booking progress. When you finish the questions on our booking page, your browser’s local storage keeps a timestamp and the reference number of your submission under the key cim.booking for 24 hours, so a page refresh takes you straight to the calendar. It is not used for tracking.
  • Embedded video. Our video player (Wistia) may set cookies or use local storage to play videos, remember playback settings and measure views.
  • Embedded forms and scheduling. Typeform and Calendly may set cookies that they need to work and to measure how their tools are used.
  • Web fonts. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver the font files.

We do not currently use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking pixels on the Site. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy first. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some embedded videos or forms may not work without them.

Where your information is stored

We are based in Canada, but some of our service providers store or process information in other countries, including the United States. When information is held outside Canada it is subject to the laws of that country, and may be accessible to its courts, law-enforcement and national-security authorities. We choose providers that protect information to a standard comparable to ours.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes above:

  • Enquiries and booking information from people who do not become clients: up to 24 months after our last contact.
  • Client records: for the length of the engagement and afterwards as long as tax, accounting and legal requirements demand (in Canada, generally six years).
  • SMS and marketing consent records: while you are subscribed, and afterwards for as long as we need to show when and how you gave or withdrew consent and to honour your opt-out.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

How we protect it

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including access controls, encrypted connections and limiting access to people who need it for their work. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we will notify you and the appropriate regulator as the law requires.

Your rights and choices

You can ask us to:

  • give you access to the personal information we hold about you and tell you how it has been used and shared;
  • correct information that is inaccurate or incomplete;
  • delete your information, unless we need to keep it for legal or contractual reasons;
  • stop sending you marketing emails or text messages.

Email hello@c-and-i-media.com with your request. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within 30 days. If you live somewhere whose law gives you additional rights (for example the EU, the UK or certain US states), contact us the same way to use them.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. The Site is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Third-party services and links

The Site embeds and links to services run by other companies, which have their own privacy policies: Typeform, Calendly, Wistia and Google (including Google Fonts and YouTube). We are not responsible for how they handle your information, so please read their policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a material change, we will tell you by email or with a notice on the Site before it takes effect.
