How can I still keep value in my videos if I don’t write the script myself?

We transcript all of your calls, 1-1 meetings, and content inside of your community, and use it for your videos, even matching your talking style. You are also free to still script your own videos.

How much time does this take on my side?

Since you won’t have to script, manage editors and thumbnail designers, or handle uploading, this will only take you about 1–2 hours a week, depending on how often you decide to upload.

When can we get started?

After on-boarding, we will have your first video ready within 1 week.

What is the free trial?

After onboarding, as long as you qualify, we offer a free video with everything done-for-you: ideation, scripting, editing and uploading — with you being able to back out at any point.

Do I need an existing audience?

No. The system is built around search and suggested traffic, not around an existing following. Starting from zero is normal.

Do you use AI to make the videos?

Your scripts are written by real people, from your own calls, community and expertise. We use tooling to speed up transcription and research — never to replace your voice.

Who is C&I Media a good fit for?

Coaches, consultants, agency owners and founders with an offer that already converts, who want a channel that books calls instead of a feed that resets every day.

What equipment do I need?

A decent camera or recent phone, a basic mic, and a window or one soft light. We’ll walk you through the exact setup on your onboarding call.
