Program description

C&I Media sends text messages to people who opt in. There are two types, and you choose each one separately:

  • Non-marketing messages: call confirmations and reminders, rescheduling notices, replies to your enquiry, and project updates for clients (for example when a video is ready for review).
  • Marketing messages: occasional offers and news about C&I Media’s services, sent only if you ticked the marketing box.

How to opt in

You opt in by entering your mobile number in the form on this page, or in another C&I Media form that shows the same SMS consent wording, and ticking one or both consent boxes. The boxes are never pre-ticked, and you do not have to tick either one to book a call or buy our services.

After you opt in, we send a confirmation text. You can opt back in at any time after opting out by replying START or by submitting the form again.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies. Non-marketing messages depend on your bookings and projects; marketing messages are occasional.

Cost

C&I Media does not charge for text messages. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile plan. Contact your carrier for details.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message at any time. We also accept STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END and QUIT. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and no further texts unless you opt in again.

You can also opt out by emailing hello@c-and-i-media.com with the number you want removed. Opting out of texts does not cancel your booking or your services.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email hello@c-and-i-media.com.
